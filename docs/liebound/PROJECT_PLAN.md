# LIEBOUND — Independent game roadmap (prototype 0.1)

**Scope:** New and separate game; never modify, replace, or redirect the existing Trap Dungeon game at `/trap-dungeon/`.

**Brand:** LIEBOUND is a working title only. Verify trademarks, search/store conflicts and domains before commercial release.

## Product
- Genre: precision platformer with a two-phase dimension-flip mechanic.
- Core differentiator: cyan and magenta platforms alternate between solid and intangible.
- Audience: players seeking short, skill-based speed-run challenges.
- Art direction: cinematic cyan-vs-magenta fantasy with readable foreground collision silhouettes.
- Controls: keyboard and touch; prompt restart; low-friction onboarding.
- Accessibility goals: reduced flashes option, color-blind distinguishable outlines/icons, adjustable input sensitivity.

## Prototype delivered
- Three authored rooms, flipping platforms, hazards, shards, portal exits, keyboard/touch controls
- Local stopwatch, death count, personal best, shareable challenge room links
- No ads, third-party telemetry, randomized purchases, network leaderboard, or in-app payments

## Before public promotion
1. Human-playtest all rooms on phone, laptop, slow Android, keyboard, and a screen reader pass.
2. Test that every jump sequence is possible and not frustrating; monitor anonymous volunteered completion/death data or local tester feedback.
3. Test social share links in chat apps (including mobile browsers and PWA), compare same level geometry.
4. Add fixed deterministic seeds for future daily challenges and server-side score validation before online leaderboards.
5. Polish game feel: jump forgiveness, landing VFX, sound, animations, camera, strong tutorial, pause and restart UI.
6. Build original brand art, small trailer capture, accessibility fixes, privacy and legal pages.
7. Verify brand naming and commercial rights to assets; release only when quality is proven.

## Organic growth — no ads or paid advertising
- Launch devlog with short, authentic clips of flips, close calls, record runs, and surprising saves.
- Share the playable demo on relevant game-dev communities per their rules and seek constructive playtest feedback, not spam.
- Invite volunteer testers using share links; hold a weekly fastest-clear challenge, no monetary prizes or wagering.
- Create community-driven levels and challenge codes, then add creator tools if there is demand.
- Work with interested content creators through voluntary coverage, not paid placements.
- Track opt-in testers: 30-second play completion, room 1 success, retries, return visits and friend challenge usage.

## Ethical monetization — no advertising
- Free web prototype for discovery and proof of fun.
- Fixed-price premium full game (tentatively USD $3–5) with clear content list.
- Optional fixed-price expansion packs after substantial new levels exist.
- No ads (including rewarded ads), pay-to-win, loot boxes, casino/roulette mechanics, gambling, or purchases designed to exploit children.
- Validate local payment processor eligibility, banking/payout availability and tax compliance before offering purchases.

## Publishing path
- Technical preview from GitHub Pages: `/trap-dungeon/liebound/` under a **new subdirectory**.
- Separate brand/repository/domain when release candidate is ready; **the current hosted preview must not replace the old homepage**.
- itch.io is a candidate for free demo and premium sale; requires owner-controlled publisher and payout accounts, tax/payment verification and compliance with current platform terms.
- Steam/Android/iOS come after proven demand and platform-specific validation.

## Current limitations
- This is a tested technical vertical slice, **not** a polished finished game.
- Online multiplayer, online leaderboard, payments, customer support and full store release are NOT implemented.
- No paid publishing account, marketing channel or third-party account has been created or connected.
