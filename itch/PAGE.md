## Description (paste into the page)

**Everything here is safe. (It is not.)**

You are a tiny miner hero in a dungeon where the signs lie, the doors run away, the flag is fake, and every trap has an opinion about your skills. Die, and the dungeon laughs at you out loud, in Arabic dialects. Then you press any key and try again, because you are very close this time. Probably.

**What you get**
- Endless levels, generated from a seed, so every level number is the same level for everyone
- Three difficulties: Normal, Crazy, Hell
- Six heroes to suffer with: Falafel, Uncle, Chicken, Grandma, Cat, Donkey
- Double jump, springs, saws, falling anvils, turrets, ghosts, fake doors and a lot of lying signs
- A boss, King Trap, every 10 levels, and a real ending at level 50 (then it keeps going, because the traps never end)
- Death cards with a taunt for each trap, spoken by voices in Levantine, Egyptian and Palestinian Arabic
- Plays on phone (touch buttons) and on a keyboard

**Controls**
Arrow keys or A/D to move, Up / W / Space to jump (press again in the air for a second jump), P pause, R restart, M mute. On a phone: the three on-screen buttons.

**Heads up**
The game text and voices are in Arabic right now. You can still play without reading it, the traps speak for themselves. An English version is planned.
